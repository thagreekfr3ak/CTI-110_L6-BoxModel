Damnation

Damnation(22 April 2003) is Opeths softer, more atmospheric album, focusing heavily on clean vocals, acoustic guitars, melancholy melodies, and progressive rock influences. Its sound is calm, emotional, and haunting, without the harsh death-metal style heard on Opeth’s other albums.

Fun Fact! Åkerfeldt has said that the making of Deliverance and Damnation was one of the worst times of his life. He stayed up all night desperately composing ideas, then tracked them with his bandmates during the day.

Deliverance

Deliverance(12 November 2002), by contrast, is much heavier and more aggressive, featuring false chord vocals, death metal guitar riffs, and complex drumming.

"While Damnation predicted the post-growl Opeth, Deliverance built the foundations of the more immediate future." -Guitar.com
